About

Sampling roadshows, run from one place

We put sales promoters into supermarkets and grocers to run brand activations. RoadCrew replaces the spreadsheet that used to hold it all — who is working, which outlet, which days — and gives every promoter their own schedule on their phone.

Schedule

One calendar, every outlet

Month and list views, brand-coloured, conflict warnings. A run that overlaps a promoter’s other booking is flagged before it is saved, not discovered on the day.

Team

Promoters by region, one tap to assign

Promoters by region, active or inactive, one tap to assign. Each promoter sees only their own days, on their own phone, with the outlet and shift for each one.

Proof

A photo from the booth, seen live

Promoters check in with a photo from the outlet, coordinators see it live. Every activation carries its own evidence, dated and timed, without a single phone call.
